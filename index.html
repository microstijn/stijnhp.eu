Stijn Peeters

Wageningen University

I work across microbiology, biogeochemistry, ecological theory and the philosophy of biology. My work runs from culturing novel bacteria to models of pathogen transport, water quality and resilience.

Email ORCID GitHub

Current work

Pathogen transport & early warning
Mechanistic prediction of norovirus contamination from sewer overflows to estuarine shellfish, with an operational forecast (PreVir).
Ecological resilience
Energy-budget models of how organisms lose and regain the capacity to recover after disturbance.
Genome architecture
Quantifying how prokaryotic genomes are organised across thousands of genomes: gene overlap, operons, strandedness (genomeArch).
Reproducible pathogen modelling
A toolkit and learning materials for waterborne-pathogen modelling, from data to models to insight (WATERPATH).
Philosophy of biology
Rethinking bacterial “programmed cell death” as immune defence, and what a biological function is for.
